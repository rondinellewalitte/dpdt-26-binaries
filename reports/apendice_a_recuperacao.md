# Outcome of the recovery pass, per target

(Appendix A of the manuscript until the submitted version; moved to the deposit when the manuscript was cut to 20 pages. Nothing is changed: Table 2 of the paper carries the counts, this file carries the per-target outcome.)


Of the eight targets whose period ladder had not closed in the main batch, intermediate 2-min sectors were fetched from MAST (96 light curves; 21–23 sectors each for the four in the northern continuous-viewing zone, 1–3 for the others) and the batch was rerun. Of those eight, six still fail the ladder in the rerun, with median half-sector bars of 1.0–4.1 min (the tolerance is three times the median bar, 3–12 min here) that the cycle count cannot bridge without an outlier-sector rule that does not exist. One more stays in the row for a different reason: TIC 128790976 closed its ladder with the added sectors under the earlier TESS bar and then failed phase coverage, and with the bar of Section 3.3 its tolerance shrinks to 6.4 min, so the ladder no longer closes and the target remains in row 4, where the coverage failure would meet it next. One leaves: TIC 353871287 now stops earlier, at the 500-point floor of its SuperWASP curve (row 1). And one enters from outside the row: TIC 159638876, whose run had crashed in the main batch before the ladder — the supplementary manifest was read twice, the duplicated sectors had zero spread and an undefined weight, and the failure was listed then as a catalogue period error — now reaches the ladder and fails it with a median half-sector bar of 19 min. Six plus one, minus one, plus one: **row 4 holds eight targets in the rerun**, the same eight as in Table 2 (TIC 128790976, 159638876, 166606391, 206544316, 353939279, 356896550, 359675849 and 469979014).

Of the six without an applicable eclipse duration (row 5), one is a contact binary (morphology 0.99, catalogue width 36.6 h for P = 4.8 d) that the trapezoid does not describe and was not attempted. For the five others the eclipse width was measured on the folded TESS curve; for two of them the estimator returned exactly its 35%-of-period ceiling. That value is not a measurement of T14 — it is the estimator failing to locate a minimum when the curve is folded on the *catalogue* period, which points to the catalogue period rather than to the duration. These two are recorded as "minimum not found at the published period", a different statement from "duration not published", and one that would send a follow-up to the period, not to the eclipse. None of the six was recovered.
