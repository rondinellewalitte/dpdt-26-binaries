# Readings withdrawn between versions (items 1–16)

(Part of Appendix D of the manuscript until the submitted version; moved to the deposit when the manuscript was cut to 20 pages. The paper keeps the record of the changes of state and the items about defects of the publication chain, 17–21, which its own text discusses; these sixteen are the readings withdrawn between versions.)

1. *A median reduced χ² of 1.41 read as bars 1.39 times too small,* and the archival variance factor of 2.73 read as *SuperWASP bars 1.65 times too small*, with a "corrected" set of ten detections and a "conservative joint correction" (SuperWASP ×1.65, TESS ×1.34) leaving eight. The composition error of Section 5.2.2 — the standard error of the season mean assigned to each season as its bar — accounted for a factor of 1.74; corrected, the factor is 1.35 and the median 0.67, and the corrections are withdrawn.
2. *The remaining excess as small-sample bias.* The expectation written for the null test of Section 5.2.3 was a SuperWASP null of 1.3–1.8 from the ν/(ν − 2) argument; the null is 0.77, because r and s are the same numbers, and the measured 1.35 is an excess located in the three targets with a rejected quadratic.
3. *The 23 adequate targets against an unconditioned null.* A version compared the residuals of the 23 targets that passed the goodness-of-fit cut with a null simulated on the same 23 designs without the cut (0.78; TESS 0.96, the measured 0.55 at its 13th percentile). With the cut applied inside the null the TESS null is 0.70 and the measured value is at the 23rd percentile (Section 5.2.3); the same conditioning was propagated to the injection of Section 5.2.4, where the 23 had likewise been compared without the cut.
4. *"z² = 0.5 by construction" as the reason for the null of 0.77.* A version explained the null of Section 5.2.3 by the tied residual and bar of a two-season pair; the leverage-corrected ratio of such a pair is exactly 1, and the null below 1 comes from the floors on the bar (an external check; Section 5.2.3).
5. *A TESS-side bound of f ≲ 0.5 on the in-window tertiary fraction,* and the reading that orbits at f = 0.24 explain 42% of the archival excess, both obtained by injecting with the bars held fixed and comparing with a measurement whose estimator re-estimates them. Replaced by the injection with the matched estimator, whose TESS-side bound (f ≳ 0.8 excluded) is uninformative, and an archival side nearly blind to orbits (Section 5.2.4).

**Transfers from one object or one population to another.**

6. *A borrowed efficiency.* A version multiplied survey-detected tertiary fractions by an efficiency of 0.83 taken from the V527 Dra injection; the fractions are already convolved with each survey's efficiency, and the efficiency of one orbit at one sampling pattern does not transfer. Replaced by the per-target ε on a declared population and the in-window fraction of Tokovinin et al. (Section 5.4).
7. *"One in nine verified".* A version quoted a false-positive rate of one in nine against the literature; seven of the nine had no record long enough to test. Replaced by an existence proof and an injection rate (Section 5.4).
8. *The residuals as a bound on the transferred chain bias.* A version stated that the SuperWASP residuals of the 23 adequate targets, showing no excess, left no room for a shape-dependent bias larger than the bars. A bias common to a target's seasons is a block offset that the quadratic absorbs; the residual test cannot see it, and the only bound is the two CV Dra minima of Section 5.5 (Section 3.4).

**Criteria that measured something other than what they were named for.**

9. *A fixed flag χ²_red ≥ 2 as a validation.* An early version flagged 7 of 26 targets by a fixed reduced χ² and read the flag on V527 Dra as a blind validation; the flag's false-alarm probability depends on the degrees of freedom (0.157 at 1, 0.092 at 4), a coincidence with 7 of 26 flagged had a 27% prior probability, and the criterion was replaced by p_gof < 0.05 (Section 3.5).
10. *TESS bars "calibrated" at 1.0 min yr⁻¹.* A version called the TESS bars inflated by the secondary drift calibrated because they brought the median reduced χ² to 0.73; the coincidence came from re-weighting, and the inflation is a ceiling (Section 5.3.2).
11. *Eleven detections and nine adequate ones.* TIC 359552377 left the set when the composition error was corrected (Section 4; p_adv 0.05 → 0.22). Its departure has two readings that the data do not separate: a spurious detection removed by the corrected bars, or a real term of the size it carries (−0.25 ± 0.08 s yr⁻¹) that the gate on this design — five epochs, archival bars of 16 min — passes in 26% of injections (Section 5.2.5). The count is the same under both; what it means is not.
12. *Numbers carried over from the version before the composition correction.* An external check found, after that correction, the coefficients of Section 5.5 (HD 202042, CV Dra, V564 Dra) and the z of the VarAstro comparison still at their earlier values, a Table 5 sentence with a target count and a p_curv path that its own table did not give, "1.0 and 2.6 min yr⁻¹" called the extremes of a drift whose extremes are 0.67 and 2.59, a Table 2 one batch behind the records, and a claim that the calibrators' dispersion guard was applied to the targets; each is corrected in place (Sections 2, 3.4, 5.3.2, 5.5, Appendix A).
13. *A TESS bar too large by √2, and the sets of ten and eight built on it.* Up to the previous version the halves term of the TESS bar was |t_a − t_b|/√2, the dispersion of one half given to the whole sector (Section 3.3); an external check found it, a simulation with an independent truth confirmed it, and the chain and every test of Section 5 were rerun with |t_a − t_b|/2 (expectation written and committed before rerunning: the control unchanged, the same 26, one detection added, the TESS residual factor 1.02 → 1.59, the TESS null of the 26 rising to 1.1–1.6). The control reproduced; the same 26 were measured; TIC 390021728 entered the detected set (p_adv 0.10 → 0.013) and none left, so D10 and D8 became D11 and D9; the median reduced χ² moved from 0.67 to 0.70 and the aggregate from 1.20 to 1.47, which is above the co-estimated null where 1.20 was at its 93rd percentile — the two flagged targets that hold the TESS excess (Section 5.2.3) gained χ² as their TESS bars shrank; the TESS residual factor rose from 1.02 to 1.59 and its null from 0.96 to 0.98 (below the expected 1.1–1.6: the TESS null is set by the formal-error floor, which the smaller halves term reaches more often — `src/nulo_pisos_26.py` gives 1.01 with that floor alone under either divisor), so the TESS side of the 26 went from no excess to a mild one at the 94th percentile, and over the 23 adequate targets from the 23rd to the 24th percentile of its conditioned null; a sixth secondary drift became significant (TIC 115244268); Σε rose from 6.1 to 6.4; the 50% completeness moved from 0.015 to 0.014 s yr⁻¹; and one target of Table 2 moved from the coverage row to the ladder row (Appendix A). Every number of the earlier bar that the text still quotes is labelled as such. The null simulations could not have found this: they generated the halves and re-estimated the bar under the same rule, and returned 0.96 for a bar that was too large by √2.
14. *"The eleven detections are carried by the TESS timings, all but one vanishing under bars inflated by the secondaries' drift."* Superseded rather than withdrawn: that inflation is a ceiling, and the noise model now applied to the design is the one admitted by its own goodness of fit on the extended block, under which 7 of the 11 survive (Section 5.3.2).
15. *"TIC 232634196 has a curvature of −0.215 s yr⁻¹ over 16–20 yr."* The coefficient stands as a coefficient of that window; the secular reading does not: its own complete TESS block gives −0.65 ± 0.13 s yr⁻¹ over 2019–2025 and an 8.5-min excursion that no parabola describes (Section 5.5).
16. *"At most one or two of the eleven detections are undersampled orbits (f × Σε = 1.5)",* with the in-window tertiary fraction counted loosely (10 of 42). Replaced by the strict count of the tertiary of the inner pair (8 of 42), the long-period bands and the injection on correlated noise: N = 1.25 (95% Jeffreys 0.80–2.46; Sections 5.4 and 5.2.4).

17. *"In two of the eight targets an anticorrelated spot pattern is excluded as the principal cause of the wander"* (the reading of the first version of Section 5.3.1 to quote a fraction). The bound on the anticorrelated component was divided by the excursion of the primary's yearly means about a **linear** ephemeris of the block, which carries the block's own secular term: in TIC 232634196 that denominator is 12.36 min, of which the parabola accounts for most, against 5.02 min about a quadratic fitted to the same block. Against the second denominator the bound is 63–535% of the wander, below it in 2 of the 8 rather than 5 and below half in none, so no target has an anticorrelated pattern excluded as its principal cause. The bound itself did not change; only what it was a fraction of. Replaced by the statement that the component is bounded and, in six of the eight, bounded too loosely to exclude anything (Section 5.3.1).
